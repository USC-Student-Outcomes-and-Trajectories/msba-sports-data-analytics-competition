# MSBA Program Requirements & Academic Alignment

This document outlines the Master of Science in Business Analytics (MSBA) program requirements, curriculum alignment, and student engagement framework for the **2027 USC Marshall Basketball Analytics Competition (Virtual Edition)**.

---

## 🎓 1. Program Academic Mandate
The USC Marshall MSBA Program serves as the academic home and institutional host of the hackathon. Operating under a **zero-budget model**, the virtual hackathon provides a high-impact experiential learning vehicle that bridges classroom theory in advanced statistics, predictive modeling, and data engineering with professional sports industry challenges on a globally broadcast stage.

### 📚 Coursework Integration
The case studies and computational requirements directly leverage concepts taught in core MSBA courses:
* **DSO 510 (Business Analytics):** Translating data findings into business value and structured communication for front-office executives.
* **DSO 530 (Applied Regression Analysis):** Validating model assumptions, regression diagnostic testing, and teammate synergy estimations.
* **DSO 570 (Applied Modern Statistical Learning):** Implementing regularized models, ensemble methods, and validation techniques for basketball tracking datasets.

### 🎯 Key Learning Outcomes
Participants are expected to demonstrate proficiency in:
1. **Reproducible Data Pipelines:** Developing clean, self-documenting codebases in GitHub that allow third parties to run models out-of-the-box.
2. **Advanced Modeling:** Applying spatial-temporal tracking, play-by-play synergy metrics, or advanced regression to evaluate player efficiency.
3. **Executive Communication:** Presenting highly technical analytics in an intuitive, visual, and actionable format suited for NBA coaching staffs and general managers during a live virtual broadcast.

## 👥 2. USC Marshall Candidate Screening Process
Since the [Official Competition Rulebook](file:///Users/milanmiric/Documents/Admin%20Work%20Documents/MSBA%20-%20Baseketball%20Case%20Competition/public_documents/competition_rulebook.md) restricts each participating school to a **maximum of 2 teams** (4–5 graduate students per team), the MSBA administration has established a competitive internal nomination process.

```mermaid

    A["Phase 1: Application Lock (Nov 13, 2026)"] -->|Resume & GitHub Portfolio Screening| B["Phase 2: Internal Hackathon (Nov 20-22, 2026)"]
    B -->|Top 4 Teams Shortlisted| C["Phase 3: Selection Presentations (Dec 04, 2026)"]
    C -->|Final Roster Lock (Dec 18, 2026)| D["Nominated Team 1 & Team 2"]
```

### 📋 Phase 1: Portfolio & Academic Review
* **Eligibility:** Open to all currently enrolled USC Marshall MSBA, MBA, or quantitative PhD/MS students.
* **Requirements:** Submission of student resumes, cumulative GPA (minimum 3.3 required), and a link to a public GitHub repository showcasing prior statistical or machine learning coursework.
* **Deadline:** **November 13, 2026**.

### 💻 Phase 2: 48-Hour Internal Mini-Hackathon
* **Setup:** Shortlisted candidates are organized into self-selected teams of 4–5 students. 
* **Task:** Teams receive a historical, anonymized NBA player tracking sample set. They have 48 hours to build a predictive model answering a sample question (e.g., estimating defensive shot disruption).
* **Deliverables:** A 3-page executive summary and a 5-minute recorded video presentation.
* **Dates:** **November 20 – November 22, 2026**.

### 🎙️ Phase 3: Selection Presentations
* **Format:** The top 4 internal teams present live to a panel of USC Marshall faculty and MSBA student officers.
* **Evaluation:** Rubric focuses on reproducibility, mathematical soundness, and presentation polish.
* **Selection:** The top 2 scoring teams are officially nominated by the Program Director on **December 4, 2026**, allowing ample time for the formal registration lock on **December 18, 2026**.

## 🤝 3. Student Volunteer & Broadcast Leadership Framework
To maximize student exposure to visiting NBA executives and participating schools, the MSBA program coordinates student digital broadcast leadership roles.

### Broadcast Leadership Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Headcount | Key Responsibilities |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Zoom Webinar Co-Hosts** | 2 | Manage panelist entry, camera/mic permissions, and team transitions during the live broadcast. |
| **YouTube Livestream Producer** | 2 | Monitor stream health, manage OBS/RTMP feeds, and archive video on the USC Marshall YouTube channel. |
| **Live Chat Moderators** | 2 | Moderate public comments and curate questions from the YouTube audience for the closing panel. |
| **Timekeepers & Stage Managers** | 2 | Track presentation timing (15-min alerts, 10-min Q&A) via visual on-screen timers. |
| **Digital Marketing & Media Officers** | 2 | Capture highlight clips and manage social media announcements on LinkedIn and Twitter. |

### Recognition & Incentives
* All student leaders receive official MSBA Leadership Certificates of Recognition.
* Student broadcast leads participate directly in the private green room session with NBA judges prior to the live broadcast.
