## 🎯 The Prompt & Deliverables

* **The Prompt:** Create an analytics solution (single live dashboard or interactive graphic) that coaches or basketball executives can intuitively understand at a glance to make actionable decisions during a game.
* **Open-Ended Strategy:** Focus on solving a meaningful, real-time coaching problem (e.g., dynamic player decision quality, fatigue & substitution optimization, or situational foul risk).
* **Required Deliverables:**
  1. **Live Interactive Web Demo:** Hosted application (Streamlit, Dash, Shiny, Vercel, Observable, Tableau Public, etc.) allowing judges to interactively pick a particular game, time, or situation to evaluate situational relevance. Must only use provided data.
  2. **Reproducible GitHub Repository:** Documented codebase with complete build instructions, data pipelines, and full transparency on any AI tools utilized.
* **Evaluation Criteria:**
  * **Clarity of Visual (30%):** Ease of interpretability and instant cognitive understanding.
  * **Actionability (35%):** Direct translation into an immediate coaching or bench decision.
  * **Feasibility (20%):** Operational viability under live, high-speed game conditions.
  * **Technical Rigor & Defense (15%):** Quality of the tool, code structure, and the team's ability to defend their methodology before NBA judges.
* **AI Tool Policy:** Students are **allowed and encouraged to use AI tools**, evaluated on the quality and clarity of the tools they produce and their ability to defend them.
