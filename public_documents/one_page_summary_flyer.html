MSBA Sports Data Analytics Competition

Organized by the University of Southern California, Marshall School of Business
Supported by the MS in Business Analytics (MSBA) Program
The USC Marshall School of Business is proud to host the inaugural 2027 MSBA Sports Data Analytics Competition. Graduate student teams analyze NBA play-by-play data to develop a live, interactive analytics tool (single dashboard or visual) empowering coaches and executives to make real-time, actionable decisions during games.

👥 Eligibility & Application
  • Who is Eligible to Apply: Students currently enrolled in graduate programs with a focus on business, entertainment, sports, and analytics (e.g., MSBA, MBA, Data Science, Quantitative MS/PhD).
  • Team Structure: Teams of 4 to 5 graduate students (each invited school may nominate up to 2 teams).
  • Cost: 100% Free Participation
  • School Application: Submit institutional interest via the link below:
    Expression of Interest in Participation (Link: xxx) →
📂 Process & Deliverables
  • Roster Collection & Launch: Organizers reach out to registered programs to request confirmed names of team members and share launch details in advance.
  • Deliverables: A Live Interactive Dashboard (hosted demo link with situational filtering) and a GitHub Repository with code & documentation. No static slide decks.
  • Evaluation & AI: Evaluated on visual clarity, decision actionability, feasibility, and technical defense. AI tools are permitted and encouraged.
  • Judging Panel: Direct live evaluation and Q&A with leading analytics professionals from the sports industry (NBA).

2027 Event is Virtual. To ensure a viable scale for the event, we request schools to register their interest using the form above. Organizers will follow up with a registration link for schools to submit their rosters and confirm participation.

💡 Note for Students: Students should reach out to their institution (faculty or program directors) and have them register for the event.
