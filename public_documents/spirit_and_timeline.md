## 🌟 Spirit & Goals of the Competition
The Marshall School of Business at the University of Southern California is hosting the inaugural **MSBA Sports Data Analytics Competition** for graduate students enrolled in business analytics, data science, and MBA programs. This competition provides a high-stakes, virtual opportunity for students to tackle real-world basketball problems and present their solutions live to leading NBA industry professionals.

USC's primary goal is to bridge academic excellence with elite front-office and coaching decision-making, bringing together the nation's leading analytical talent with active sports executives on a globally broadcast platform.

Los Angeles serves as a premier global sports hub, home to **12 major professional sports teams**:
* **Basketball (NBA/WNBA):** Los Angeles Lakers, Los Angeles Clippers, Los Angeles Sparks
* **Baseball (MLB):** Los Angeles Dodgers, Los Angeles Angels
* **Football (NFL):** Los Angeles Rams, Los Angeles Chargers
* **Soccer (MLS/NWSL):** Los Angeles FC (LAFC), LA Galaxy, Angel City FC
* **Hockey (NHL):** Los Angeles Kings, Anaheim Ducks

This ecosystem is further enriched by top-tier collegiate athletics programs at USC and UCLA, establishing Southern California as an ideal epicenter for sports analytics innovation.

---

## 👥 Roster & Nomination Rules
* **Invitation-Only:** This is an exclusive, invitation-based competition. 
* **Team Capacity:** Invited schools are permitted to nominate **up to two project teams** consisting of **4 to 5 graduate students** (enrolled in MSBA, MBA, or related quantitative/data science programs).
* **Nomination Process:** Each invited school may determine its own internal nomination process to select its two representative teams.
* **Nomination Deadline:** Participating schools must confirm their participation and submit their nominated student rosters by **December 18, 2026**.
