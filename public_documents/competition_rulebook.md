# Official Competition Rulebook

## 📜 1. Introduction & Objectives
The **USC Marshall Basketball Analytics Competition** is designed to challenge students to apply cutting-edge data science, statistical modeling, and economic analysis to real-world basketball problems. Competitors will analyze proprietary and open-source data to generate insights valuable to NBA front-office executives.

---

## 👥 2. Roster Rules & Eligibility
* **Student Status:** Open to active undergraduate and graduate students enrolled in accredited universities.
* **Team Size:** Exactly **3 to 4 students** per team.
* **School Cap:** To ensure a highly competitive and diverse pool, a **maximum of 2 teams** will be accepted per university.
* **Roster Lock:** Roster changes are permitted until the registration deadline on **December 4, 2026 (11:59 PM PST)**. After this date, rosters are locked, and substitutions are only allowed under extreme extenuating circumstances approved by the committee.

---

## 📂 3. Submission Deliverables
Teams are evaluated based on a dual submission consisting of a presentation deck and their underlying code repository.

### A. Presentation Deck Requirements
* **Format:** Single PDF document.
* **Page Limit:** Maximum **15 slides** (including title and appendix slides). Any pages exceeding the 15-page limit will be discarded and not evaluated.
* **Design:** Professional formatting, legible charts, and clear visualizations are expected.
* **Anonymity Rule (CRITICAL):** To ensure a completely unbiased blind evaluation, **Round 1 slide decks must contain NO references to school names, colors, logos, student names, or initials**. Submissions containing identifying markers will face immediate point deductions or disqualification.

### B. Technical Code Repository Requirements
* **Hosting:** Public GitHub repository (or private repository shared with committee accounts).
* **Reproducibility:** The codebase must contain:
  1. A `README.md` describing how to run the pipeline, the dependencies, and the main model details.
  2. A dependency file (e.g., `requirements.txt`, `environment.yml`, or `pyproject.toml`).
  3. Clean, documented code (Python, R, or Julia) that executes without syntax errors.
  4. Script outputs or cached results that allow faculty judges to reproduce the model figures and tables.

---

## ⏳ 4. Submission & Late Policies
* **Submission Deadline:** **January 4, 2027 (11:59 PM PST)**.
* **Portal:** Upload via the USC Marshall competition portal.
* **Late Penalty:** 
  * Up to 12 hours late: 15% flat deduction.
  * 12–24 hours late: 30% flat deduction.
  * Over 24 hours late: Submissions will not be accepted or graded.

---

## ⚖️ 5. Academic Integrity & Professionalism
* **Originality:** All work must be the original work of the registered team members.
* **External Help:** Teams may consult public resources, research papers, and software libraries. However, consulting with professors, NBA team staff, or industry mentors regarding the specific case prompt is strictly prohibited.
* **Use of AI:** Generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude) are permitted as writing assistants or debugging tools, but the underlying analytics, modeling design, and strategic insights must be driven by the students. Teams must document any substantial use of AI in their repository's `README.md`.
