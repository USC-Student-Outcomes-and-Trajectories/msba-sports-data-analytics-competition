## 🌟 Spirit of the Competition
The USC Marshall Basketball Analytics Competition bridges academic rigor with elite front-office decision-making. By restricting registration to exactly two teams per school, the tournament ensures a highly competitive, concentrated field of top-tier talent. 

The competition challenges students to solve a complex, data-driven basketball problem under realistic pressure. The two-stage format pairs unbiased academic evaluation with high-stakes critique from active NBA analytics professionals, offering students unparalleled exposure and giving NBA franchises exclusive access to the next generation of analytical talent.

## 📅 External Team Deadlines
*These are all candidate-facing deadlines leading up to NBA All-Star Weekend.*

* **November 1, 2026:** Registration Portal Opens. Formal university invitations distributed.
* **December 4, 2026:** Registration Deadline. Team rosters must be locked (3–4 students per team, max 2 teams per school).
* **December 11, 2026:** **Case Prompt & Data Launch**. Data sets made public to registered competitors via the Virtual "Tip-Off Event".
* **January 4, 2027:** **Round 1 Submissions Due**. Teams must upload their maximum 15-slide PDF presentation deck and official GitHub repository links.
* **January 15, 2027:** Finalist Announcement. Top 3 to 5 teams are formally invited to the live presentation round at USC Marshall.
* **February 5, 2027:** Final Presentation Deck Lock. Finalists submit their polished presentations; no further code or slide edits are permitted after this date.
* **February 12, 2027 (Friday of NBA All-Star Weekend):** **The Main Event**. Live final presentations and Q&A with NBA executives at USC Marshall.

## 🛠️ Internal Committee & Data Timeline
*This schedule outlines when internal organizational structures are created, data pipelines are cleared, and behind-the-scenes evaluation takes place.*

### 1. Committee Formation & Case Scoping Phase (September 1 – October 15, 2026)
* **September 10, 2026:** Form Core Organizing Pillars. All chairs and student committee roles filled for:
    * *Analytics & Case Development*
    * *Outreach & Judge Liaison*
    * *Operations & Logistics*
* **September 20, 2026:** Budget finalized and submitted to the USC Marshall Dean's Office / corporate sponsors for approval.
* **October 1, 2026:** **Data Asset Lock**. The Analytics team secures data partnerships or cleans open-source pipelines (e.g., NBA API, Sportradar). Data sets are kept strictly confidential within this internal team.
* **October 15, 2026:** Official competition rulebook and landing page asset finalized.

### 2. Recruiting & Logistics Phase (October 16 – November 30, 2026)
* **November 1, 2026:** Launch registration portal. Outreach team begins school communications.
* **November 15, 2026:** Preliminary Round Faculty Panel locked. Onboard internal and external faculty judges on blind review parameters.
* **November 30, 2026:** NBA Finals Judging Panel locked. Secure written commitments from 3–5 active NBA analytics professionals.

### 3. Case Launch & Preliminary Evaluation Phase (December 1, 2026 – January 15, 2027)
* **December 11, 2026:** **Data Released Externally**. Analytics team pushes datasets to GitHub and launches the virtual tip-off webinar.
* **January 5, 2027:** Submissions sorted and anonymized. Operations team strips all university names/logos from slide decks and GitHub code to assign random numeric IDs.
* **January 5 – January 14, 2027:** **Faculty Evaluation Windows**. Faculty panel reviews submissions using the standardized model reproducibility grading rubric.
* **January 15, 2027:** Committee tallies faculty scores and coordinates logistics with the chosen 3–5 finalist teams.

### 4. Event Execution Phase (January 16 – February 12, 2027)
* **January 22, 2027:** Operations team coordinates venue spaces (e.g., Popovich Hall or Fertitta Hall), catering, and AV technology schedules.
* **February 5, 2027:** **NBA Judge Packets Sent**. Finalist decks and technical summaries are securely delivered to the NBA judging panel for pre-reading.
* **February 12, 2027:** Competition Day execution.
